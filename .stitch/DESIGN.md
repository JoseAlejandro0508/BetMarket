# ApuestasDeportivas Stitch Design System

Project: `projects/11309137477063861572`
Theme source: Stitch project `ApuestasDeportivas 1xBet Style`

## North Star
Premium sportsbook dark UI with high-contrast data readability, tonal layering, glass surfaces, and no hard 1px separators as primary structure.

## Core Tokens

### Typography
- Family: `Inter`
- Headline: `Inter`
- Body: `Inter`
- Label: `Inter`

### Color Roles
- Background: `#001428`
- Surface low: `#061d31`
- Surface container: `#0b2136`
- Surface high: `#172b41`
- Surface highest: `#22364c`
- Primary: `#a7c8ff`
- Primary container: `#3991fa`
- Secondary: `#a4c9f9`
- Tertiary accent: `#a4d65c`
- On surface text: `#d1e4ff`
- Muted text: `#c1c6d5`
- Outline: `#8b919e`

### Shape & Elevation
- Roundness: `ROUND_FOUR`
- Cards and controls use soft rounded corners and ambient deep-blue shadow.
- Glass overlays use blur and subtle transparency.

## Composition Rules
- Use tonal shifts and spacing for separation; avoid hard dividers.
- Keep betting actions high-contrast and obvious.
- Navigation is lateral and expandable where possible.
- Profile/avatar menu includes theme toggle, settings access, and sign out.
